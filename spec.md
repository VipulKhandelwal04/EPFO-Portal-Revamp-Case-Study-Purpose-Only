# Spec: Govt Case Study

**Problem:** Citizens hit system-design failures across government services — false closure (marked "resolved" without action), rejection loops, silent stalls, jurisdiction ping-pong, and an agent/bribe economy that sells certainty the system won't give for free.
**Users:** Citizens interacting with government services. No single candidate chosen yet — current front-runners are EPF withdrawal claimants (strongest evidence) and RTO driving-licence renewal.
**In scope (what we promise):** One real product around one validated pain, chosen from the 18-candidate board; primary research survey (live); social listening; international-analogue research.
**Out of scope:** Anti-bribery/corruption-fighting as the product frame (decision [2026-08-28](decisions/2026-08-28-system-design-not-anti-bribery.md)); filing/tracking/account management on the current EPF framing.
**Success metric:** none yet — discovery phase, not converged on a direction.
**Constraints:** DPDP compliance. CPGRAMS discloses complainant identity, so any public-facing feature must protect anonymity. Citizen-side only, no official API access (bring-your-own-data pattern). Published artifacts follow a zero-em-dash, plain-language rule.
