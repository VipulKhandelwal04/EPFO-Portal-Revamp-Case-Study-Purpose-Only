# Dealing With Government Offices: Your Experience

**Purpose:** Primary research for the government and public-sector case study. The case-study brief: pick an existing problem that affects citizens, find an inefficient government process, or improve how citizens interact with government — and build a real product around it. This survey lets citizens rank those problems by lived pain instead of the problem being chosen from desk research. No candidate process is pre-decided; the answers pick it.

**From:** Vipul (building a free citizen-facing product as part of a product case study)
**To:** Anyone in India who has dealt with a government office, website, or process in roughly the last 2 years
**How your answers will be used:** Read in aggregate to pick one problem to solve. Individual answers stay anonymous and are never published with identifying details.

## Form setup notes (for you, not respondents; delete before sending)

- Google Forms settings: do NOT collect email addresses, do NOT limit to 1 response (that forces sign-in), do NOT make anything except the two Section-1 questions required. Anonymous forms get honest answers about agents and extra payments.
- Map each question below to the field type in parentheses. One Google Form section per `##` heading; the section intro text is provided under each heading.
- Plain-language rule: no abbreviations or scheme names a first-time respondent might not know. Where an official term is unavoidable (e.g., RTI), explain it inline in everyday words.
- Estimated completion: 6 to 8 minutes. Say so in the form description; it raises completion rates.

- Optional: a Hindi version of the same questions roughly doubles reachable respondents on WhatsApp. Worth doing only after the English form proves the questions work.
