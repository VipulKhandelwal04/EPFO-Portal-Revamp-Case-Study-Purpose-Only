# Frame the product as system design, not anti-bribery

**Decision:** The product must not focus on resolving bribery/corruption directly. It targets system design instead — completeness-first checklists, status legibility, ownership routing, verifiable closure, safe (anonymous) escalation. Survey bribe questions stay as diagnostics only.
**Context:** Social listening surfaced bribery/the agent economy as the most visible, most-quoted citizen pain.
**Options considered:** Build the product around fighting corruption directly; or build around the structural gaps that make bribery the rational choice.
**Why this one (the tradeoff, one line):** Traded attacking the most visible symptom for building something a citizen-side tool can actually deliver — bribery is a willingness-to-pay signal for certainty the system won't give for free, not a fixable target without enforcement power.
**Amends spec?** yes — sets Out of scope.
**Revisit when:** Evidence emerges that legibility/routing fixes alone don't move the bribery rate (precedent cited: Passport Seva's PSK step went bribe-free by becoming legible/trackable, not through anti-corruption enforcement).
